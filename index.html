Furniture drawn for your walls

Kitchens, wardrobes and whole walls of wood, made to measure by Kencho Group in our own workshop in Tbilisi, Georgia. Nothing here comes off a shelf: we measure your room, draw every millimetre, build it in our workshop and fit it ourselves.

What we make

  • Kitchens: islands, long runs, tall walls of storage. Every door cut for your room.
  • Wardrobes: floor to ceiling, mirrored, caned or plain. Built into the wall, not stood against it.
  • Media walls: one clean wall for the TV, the speakers and the cables.
  • Shelving: arched, fluted, lit from inside.
  • Bedrooms and kids rooms: panelled walls, beds with storage, a climbing wall.
  • Offices: walnut walls and fitted storage, built around the working day.

Pick a palette

Five kitchen palettes to start from: beige white and black quartz, sage and oak, forest green with walnut and marble, warm white with honey oak and travertine, and greige with graphite.
