The Room by Kencho Group

An interactive living room and kitchen by Kencho Group, makers of custom kitchens, wardrobes and furniture in Tbilisi, Georgia. Two ways in: the original black-and-white opening with a narrator and kinetic type, which ends in a realistic room in beige white and black, or the retro sketchbook, which ends in a hand-drawn room in colour.
